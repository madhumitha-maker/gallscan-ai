Diagnostic Taxonomy

9 Supported Radiological Conditions

Trained on clinical ultrasound scans encompassing benign, inflammatory, acute, and malignant biliary conditions.

Class 1

Gallstones (Cholelithiasis)

Acoustic shadowing with hyperechoic mobile or impacted calculi within the lumen.

Class 2

Abdomen & Retroperitoneum

Normal baseline parenchyma and adjacent abdominal anatomical scans.

Class 3

Cholecystitis (Acute/Chronic)

Wall thickening, distension, pericholecystic fluid, and positive sonographic Murphy sign.

Class 4

Gangrenous & Membranous

Intraluminal sloughed membranes, asymmetrical mucosal ulceration and ischemic necrosis.

Class 5

Gallbladder Perforation

Discontinuous gallbladder wall with pericholecystic abscess and peritoneal effusion.

Class 6

Polyps & Crystals

Fixed non-shadowing intraluminal mucosal projections and comet-tail reverberations.

Class 7

Adenomyomatosis

Hyperplastic mural changes with Rokitansky-Aschoff intramural cystic diverticula.

Class 8

Gallbladder Carcinoma

Malignant polypoid or infiltrating mass replacing the gallbladder lumen with hepatic invasion.

Class 9

Wall Thickening (Various)

Non-specific reactive mural edema secondary to hepatic, cardiac, or renal etiology.
